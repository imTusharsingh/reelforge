# Deprecation inventory

VideoRun is the only generation pipeline. T2a and T2b removed the manual
generation, review and publishing screens and endpoints, the M16 GENERATION
workflow, and the pipeline rollback switches. The M11 single-pass reviewer is
retired. Publishing still validates stored review evidence and render manifests.
